02 · Locum work

Locum doctors and nurses are welcome.

If you are a doctor or nurse and would like to provide services to St. Martha Hospital on a locum basis, we would be glad to hear from you. Locum professionals are contacted when the hospital needs their services.

  1. 1

    Visit the hospital office

    Come in person to the St. Martha Hospital office in Kiminini.

  2. 2

    Submit your professional documents

    Leave your documents with the office so that they can be considered.

  3. 3

    We review and get in touch

    The hospital reviews what you have submitted and contacts suitable professionals when locum opportunities arise.

Submitting your documents does not guarantee an immediate placement or a locum assignment. We will contact you when your services are required.

Locum interest is handled in person at the hospital office, so there is no online application form.
