General information

Where is St. Martha Hospital located?

St. Martha Hospital is in Kiminini, Trans Nzoia County, Kenya. For directions or help finding the hospital, please use the details on our Contact page.

What are the hospital’s operating hours?

Regular operating hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday and Sunday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. For urgent enquiries outside these hours, please contact the hospital directly.

How can I contact St. Martha Hospital?

You can reach the hospital by phone or email. Our Contact page has the current details and other ways to get in touch.

Do I need to book an appointment before visiting?

You can request an appointment through our Book Now page. If you’re not sure whether the service you need requires an appointment, please contact the hospital for guidance.

Appointments and patient services

How can I book an appointment?

Submit an appointment request through the Book Now page. Please give your contact details and your preferred appointment information.

The hospital will follow up with you about your request. A request is not a confirmed appointment until the hospital has done so.

What should I bring when visiting the hospital?

If you have them, bring your identification, relevant medical records, prescriptions, previous test results and any insurance information you may need. If you are visiting for a specific service, contact the hospital to confirm whether you need any other documents.

Can I visit the hospital on weekends?

Yes. Regular operating hours include Saturday and Sunday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. If you need to confirm that a particular service is available, please contact the hospital.

Can I request my medical records or test results?

Please contact the hospital directly to ask about accessing your medical records or test results. The hospital can guide you through the process.

Insurance and payments

Does St. Martha Hospital accept health insurance?

Yes. St. Martha Hospital accepts the Social Health Authority (SHA). If you have questions about your coverage, your eligibility or the services your plan covers, please contact the hospital before your visit.

How can I find out the cost of a consultation or treatment?

Costs depend on the service or treatment you need. Please contact the hospital directly for information about applicable charges.

Urgent care and hospital visits

What should I do if I need urgent medical attention?

Contact the hospital directly for guidance on what to do next. For a life-threatening emergency, seek immediate help from your local emergency services.

Does the hospital have an ambulance service?

Please contact the hospital directly to ask about available transport or ambulance arrangements.

Careers and locum opportunities

Are there any current job vacancies at St. Martha Hospital?

There are currently no advertised vacancies. We encourage interested applicants to check our Careers page regularly and to follow St. Martha Hospital on LinkedIn for announcements about future opportunities.

Can doctors and nurses apply for locum opportunities?

Yes. Doctors and nurses who want to provide locum services can submit their professional documents in person at the hospital office. The hospital will review submissions and contact suitable professionals when locum opportunities arise.

Submitting documents does not guarantee an immediate assignment.

General enquiries

Where can I find more information about the hospital’s services?

Visit our Services page to learn more. If you need information about a specific service, please contact the hospital directly to confirm that it is available.
